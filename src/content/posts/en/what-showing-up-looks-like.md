After a run of flaky people, someone consistent can feel almost suspicious. They text back. They confirm plans. They show up when they said they would. Where's the catch?

Often there isn't one. Calm can feel unfamiliar when you're used to chasing, and unfamiliar is easy to mistake for "no spark."

## Small things that are actually big

- They make a plan and keep it, without you having to follow up.
- They remember something you mentioned last week.
- Disagreements end with a conversation, not a disappearance.

None of this is dramatic. That's the point.
